#WomenInSTEM Pub Quiz

Our next #WomenInSTEM event is coming up, and this time it's all about bringing students together and learning how amazing women shaped the world of STEM.

Thursday, December 3rd 6:00 pm Campus Guest (U32.101), Campus Vaihingen Held in English

Join us for an evening of quizzing and connecting! Our quiz night features questions all about women in science, technology, engineering and math. Through a varied mix of questions, we want to highlight interesting statistics, the achievements of women scientists, exciting STEM facts and much more.

You'll be placed in mixed teams, giving you the chance to meet STEM students from other programs and backgrounds while competing together. We will host the quiz and guide you through the rounds, and the best teams will win great prizes!
